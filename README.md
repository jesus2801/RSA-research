# RSA Research

Cryptography is vital for data protection in an interconnected world. Methods such as RSA and ECC, based on the difficulty of factoring large numbers, are essential for digital security. However, quantum computing, which uses qubits to process information faster, presents a significant threat. Shor's quantum algorithm can factor large numbers quickly, putting systems such as RSA at risk. 

Although quantum computers are in development and have not yet demonstrated the ability to break RSA, recent studies indicate that they could be vulnerable shortly. Recent research claims that today's advanced quantum computers could crack RSA, and work is underway in post-quantum cryptography (PQC) to develop algorithms resistant to these attacks. The transition to PQC is essential to protect digital security against the emerging threats of quantum computing driven by political and unethical interests before data protection. 

## Repository

This repository hosts files used in a college machine to prove the claims made in the research. The main job of these files is to find a way to decompose a large prime number in the fastest time using languages such as Python, C, or C++.
